Freelance Booking

The Freelance Booking App Design is a UI/UX project that focuses on the user journey of discovering, scheduling, and managing freelance services through a high-contrast, accessible, and sophisticated interface. It features a streamlined onboarding and authentication flow, a dynamic dashboard for tracking bookings, categorized service discovery with ratings, an interactive booking system with instant confirmations, and comprehensive profile management. The modern Gold and White design palette reinforces a premium, concierge-style experience while keeping the interface clean and functional.
