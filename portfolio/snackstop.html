SnackStop

SnackStop is a user-focused food delivery app that makes ordering local snacks and meals fast and easy, featuring category navigation, best-seller highlights, and localized promos like “Buy 1 Take 1” with GCash payments. It incorporates local terms like “Barkada” and “Merienda” to connect with Filipino users, while offering a streamlined checkout with clear delivery fees, discounts, and payment options. The app’s earthy color palette, rounded elements, and soft shadows create a modern, approachable, and reliable visual identity.
